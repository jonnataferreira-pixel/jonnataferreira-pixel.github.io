Privacy Policy

canal-dark · Inkwell & Bones · Last updated: October 7, 2026

What this app is

canal-dark is a private tool used only by the owner of the Inkwell & Bones YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Permission to upload videos and manage captions and thumbnails on the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm the correct channel is used.

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users or viewers.

How data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than Google's own APIs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
